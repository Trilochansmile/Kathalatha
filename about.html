About

KATHALATHHA (କଥାଲଥା), The Kosli Digital Archive.

Purpose

KATHALATHHA is a digital space dedicated exclusively to preserving, presenting and documenting Kosli language, literature, folklore, oral traditions, music, history and cultural heritage. Stories, language and cultural memories deserve to be preserved.

How the archive is edited

  • The original Kosli text comes first. An English translation, where there is one, is labelled as a translation.
  • No Kosli words, translations or cultural information are invented or silently "corrected".
  • Every record states its source and its verification status.
  • A record is published only when its rights are settled and, for recordings and personal accounts, the contributor has consented.
  • Every audio and video recording has a written transcript, and every photograph has a written description.

Accessibility statement

This site is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That is a design target, not a claim of conformance: the site has not yet been tested with screen readers or by disabled users, and the list below says what is and is not in place.

  • Pages use headings and landmarks, a "Skip to main content" link, and a menu that works with a keyboard.
  • Focus is always visible. Text and controls meet the colour-contrast targets. Text can be enlarged with the browser's own zoom or text-size setting.
  • Photographs carry written descriptions. Audio and video records are meant to have transcripts, and videos captions. Where one is missing, the record says so.
  • Text in Odia script is marked with a language code. How it looks depends on a suitable Odia font being available on your device.
  • There is no CAPTCHA, no pop-up and no required registration.

If you meet a barrier, or need a record in another form, please write to us through the Contact page and say which page and what went wrong.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and its fonts are served from the site itself. Searching happens in your own browser; what you type is not sent anywhere.
