Entry helper

Fill in the boxes, press the button, and copy the result into your data file. Nothing you type here is saved or sent anywhere.

Example: media/texts/song-0001.txt

Your entry

Copy everything in the box. Then follow step 3 of the editing guide to paste it into the file.

2. Check a data file for mistakes

Open a data file on GitHub, select all the text, copy it, and paste it below. The checker tells you if a comma or a quotation mark is missing.
